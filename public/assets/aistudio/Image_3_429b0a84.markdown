## Brand & Style

This design system channels the electric vibrancy, trend-forward speed, and clean editorial precision of modern tier-one fashion e-commerce. Tailored for style-conscious consumers seeking inspiration and immediate gratification, the experience balances runway excitement with uncompromised transactional clarity.

The visual language combines **Clean Contemporary Minimalism** with **High-Energy Retail Accents**. Crisp, luminous white stages give apparel photography breathing room, while hyper-saturated magenta-pink triggers emotional impulses and highlights promotions, discounts, and calls to action. The interface maintains a light, breathable footprint that keeps visual clutter low and browsing stamina high, relying on disciplined typographic hierarchy, stark contrast, and tight structural alignment rather than decorative container bloat.

## Colors

The palette balances energetic commercial signals with rigorous legibility:

- **Primary (`#ff3f6c`)**: The energetic brand signature. Reserved for primary conversion anchors, active states, cart triggers, heart/wishlist actions, and promotional badges.
- **Secondary (`#fdf1e6`)**: A delicate warm peach tint used for subtle promotional backdrops, coupon highlights, flash deal banners, and high-value alert tags.
- **Tertiary (`#ff905a`)**: A warm coral/orange used selectively for limited-time offers, urgency counters, and secondary badges.
- **Neutral (`#282c3f`)**: Deep charcoal serving as the dominant text color, high-contrast buttons, and structural lines, avoiding the harshness of pure black.
- **Text-Muted (`#535766`)**: Secondary charcoal for subtitles, breadcrumbs, specifications, and strike-through original prices.
- **Discount Green (`#03a685`)**: Dedicated strictly to positive discount percentages, offer tags, and savings callouts.
- **Star Rating (`#14958f`)**: Crisp teal-green used for product ratings and verified badge pills.

## Typography

Typography establishes an assertive editorial attitude. Montserrat handles brand anchors, category headers, promotional callouts, and numeric prices, delivering geometric crispness and modern retail impact. Inter governs long-form content, specifications, descriptions, reviews, and utility copy for dense readability.

All headlines and brand titles feature tight tracking and uppercase styling when used as category markers or badges (`label-sm`). Price numbers utilize semi-bold to bold weights to anchor product cards, while strikethrough original prices employ regular weights in the muted text tone.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an adaptive 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`margin-desktop: 2.5rem`, `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem`) and a dynamic 2-column or 4-column layout on mobile devices (`margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`). 

Product card listings rely strictly on a 4 to 5 column density on wide desktop viewports (1280px+) to maximize browse capacity, dropping to 3 columns on tablet and 2 columns on mobile. Spacing rhythm follows 4px and 8px base multiples: micro spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) governs ratings pills, size selectors, and discount badge insets, while macro increments (`space-lg` to `space-2xl`) frame category spotlight carousels and promotional breakout banners.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a flat, razor-sharp editorial look where depth is indicated by subtle outlines and floating overlays rather than heavy shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Base canvases use pure white (`#ffffff`) or subtle neutral tint (`#f5f5f6`). Product cards sit flush with no resting shadow, separated by subtle 1px border lines (`rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.08)`).
- **Level 1 (Card Hover / Selectables)**: On hover, product cards subtly elevate using a clean directional drop shadow: `0 8px 16px rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.12)`, simultaneously revealing interactive size options or wishlist shortcuts.
- **Level 2 (Sticky Headers & Navigation)**: The top navigation bar, filter bars, and bottom action sheets employ subtle backdrop diffusion (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over a 95% opaque `#ffffff` fill, paired with a faint bottom border: `0 2px 8px rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Mega-Menus & Modals)**: Flying mega-menus, size charts, and cart sidebars cast a crisp, extended shadow: `0 16px 32px rgba(40, 44, 63, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, tailored architecture (`roundedness: 1`). Containers, buttons, cards, and input fields feature 4px corner radii (`0.25rem`), preserving a sharp, premium editorial profile without harsh corners.

Badges, chips, rating tags, and promotional stickers use fully rounded pill shapes to contrast against the structured grid of rectangular image containers. Image ratios maintain strict proportions: 3:4 for vertical fashion apparel and 1:1 for accessories and beauty items.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Bag, Checkout)**: Solid `#ff3f6c` background, `#ffffff` uppercase `label-lg` text, 4px radius, 48px standard height, subtle 2px active scaling.
- **Secondary Action (Wishlist, Secondary Nav)**: Ghost border (`1px solid #d4d5d9`), `#282c3f` text, turns `#ff3f6c` border and fill on hover.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: 3:4 vertical imagery with crisp top-left discount badges (`#ff3f6c` or `#03a685`), top-right floating circular wishlist icon.
- **Content Block**: Bold brand name (`label-md`), followed by a 1-line muted product description (`body-sm`).
- **Price Block**: Stacked inline row displaying selling price in `#282c3f` (`label-lg`), strikethrough MRP in `#535766` (`body-sm`), and calculated discount percentage in `#03a685` (`label-md`).
- **Rating Chip**: Semi-transparent floating pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`) on bottom-left of photo showing numeric rating + star glyph in `#14958f`.

### Badges & Chips
- **Offer Tag**: `#fdf1e6` background with `#ff3f6c` or `#ff905a` bold uppercase text.
- **Filter Chips**: 32px height, 1px border (`#eaeaec`), `#282c3f` text, switching to `#ff3f6c` text and light pink surface (`#fff1f4`) when selected.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search Bar**: 44px height, light gray background (`#f5f5f6`), integrated magnifying glass icon, bold charcoal text placeholder.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), 1.5px border in `#535766`, fills `#ff3f6c` with pure white checkmark when checked.

### Mega-Menu Navigation
- Full-width dropdown with multi-column categorizations (e.g., Topwear, Footwear, Indian & Festive). Bold section titles in `#ff3f6c` and sub-item list links in `#282c3f` changing to brand pink on hover.
